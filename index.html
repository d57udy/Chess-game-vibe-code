    Controls
    • Click or tap a unit, then a highlighted square.
    • Touch: 1 finger drag rotates. 2 fingers zoom, move and turn the view. Double-tap zooms to that spot, double-tap again zooms back out.
    • Mouse: drag rotates, right-drag or Shift+drag moves the view, the wheel zooms.
    • Reset view brings the camera back to your side.
    • Keyboard: arrow keys move the cursor, Enter selects or moves, Esc cancels, Space skips a fight.
