## Modifications

The files here are derived from the original glTF exports, generated by `battle3d/tools/build_assets.cjs`:

- `characters/*.glb`: animations removed (meshes, skin, embedded weapons/accessories and texture kept),
  skinned meshes moved to the scene root, meshopt compressed (`EXT_meshopt_compression` + `KHR_mesh_quantization`).
- `anims/clips.glb`, `anims/clips_skeleton.glb`: one copy of the selected animation clips on a mesh-less rig,
  tracks for unweighted IK/control bones and tracks that never leave the rest pose removed, meshopt compressed.
- `props/*`: unmodified copies of weapon/shield glTFs from both packs with their textures (`skeleton_texture.png`, `knight_texture.png`, `barbarian_texture.png`, `mage_texture.png`, `rogue_texture.png`).
- In game, `battle3d/unit-visuals.js` may recolour cloth colours of these textures at runtime (hue swap) for team readability; the files themselves are unchanged.
- Crowns, tiaras, plumes, banners, capes, auras and the base discs are procedural geometry made for this project (not KayKit assets).
- `raw/`: unmodified original character GLBs and props from both packs (build inputs and fallback placeholders).

meshopt compression. Thumbnails in `packs/*/thumbs/` and `thumbs/` are renders made by `battle3d/tools/make_thumbs.mjs`.
